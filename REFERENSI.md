### 1. Buku Teks Utama (Buku Babon DSP Internasional)
1. **Oppenheim, A. V., & Schafer, R. W. (2010).** *Discrete-Time Signal Processing* (3rd ed.). Upper Saddle River, NJ: Pearson Prentice Hall. ISBN: 978-0131988422.  
   *(Rujukan otoritatif tertinggi dunia untuk representasi sinyal diskrit, sistem LTI, sampling, Transformasi-Z, DTFT, DFT, dan desain filter FIR/IIR).*
2. **McClellan, J. H., Schafer, R. W., & Yoder, M. A. (2016).** *DSP First: A Multimedia Approach* (2nd ed.). Boston, MA: Pearson. ISBN: 978-0136019251.  
   *(Buku pegangan pembelajaran DSP modern berbasis laboratorium komputasi audio, visualisasi spektrum, dan implementasi piranti lunak).*
3. **Smith, S. W. (1997).** *The Scientist and Engineer's Guide to Digital Signal Processing*. San Diego, CA: California Technical Publishing. ISBN: 978-0966017632.  
   *(Panduan praktis terkemuka untuk insinyur teknik dan ilmuwan komputer, tersedia secara terbuka).*
4. **Proakis, J. G., & Manolakis, D. G. (2007).** *Digital Signal Processing: Principles, Algorithms, and Applications* (4th ed.). Upper Saddle River, NJ: Pearson. ISBN: 978-0131873742.  
   *(Rujukan komprehensif untuk algoritma FFT Radix-2, estimasi spektral daya, dan implementasi filter digital).*
5. **Mitra, S. K. (2011).** *Digital Signal Processing: A Computer-Based Approach* (4th ed.). New York, NY: McGraw-Hill. ISBN: 978-0073380490.
6. **Rabiner, L. R., & Schafer, R. W. (2010).** *Theory and Applications of Digital Speech Processing*. Upper Saddle River, NJ: Pearson. ISBN: 978-0136034285.

### 3. Makalah Ilmiah Klasik Bersejarah
1. **Shannon, C. E. (1949).** *Communication in the Presence of Noise*. Proceedings of the IRE, 37(1), 10-21. DOI: 10.1109/JRPROC.1949.232969.  
   *(Makalah fundamental perumusan Teorema Sampling Nyquist-Shannon).*
2. **Cooley, J. W., & Tukey, J. W. (1965).** *An Algorithm for the Machine Calculation of Complex Fourier Series*. Mathematics of Computation, 19(90), 297-301. DOI: 10.1090/S0025-5718-1965-0178586-1.  
   *(Makalah revolusioner penemuan algoritma Fast Fourier Transform).*
3. **Harris, F. J. (1978).** *On the Use of Windows for Harmonic Analysis with the Discrete Fourier Transform*. Proceedings of the IEEE, 66(1), 51-83. DOI: 10.1109/PROC.1978.10837.  
   *(Rujukan terlengkap mengenai karakteristik fungsi window: Rectangular, Hann, Hamming, Blackman, Kaiser).*
4. **Parks, T. W., & McClellan, J. H. (1972).** *Chebyshev Approximation for Nonrecursive Digital Filters with Linear Phase*. IEEE Transactions on Circuit Theory, 19(2), 189-194. DOI: 10.1109/TCT.1972.1083419.
